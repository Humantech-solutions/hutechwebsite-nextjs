Global Fashion Brand Storefront on Shopify
Establishing Shopify storefronts for international fashion brands with seamless data validation and brand-consistent design.

Project Overview
The client is an Indonesian digital solutions firm assisting global fashion brands with e-commerce setups. The initiative aimed at establishing Shopify, linking it with the client’s current elements for seamless data validation, and developing and personalizing Shopify storefronts for multiple international clothing brands. The client needed assistance in creating a brand-consistent, aesthetically pleasing storefront that could satisfy local regulations and provide a seamless purchasing experience.





Challenges
Brand Consistency
Developing a retail space that adhered to international brand standards. The storefront had to adhere closely to the defined visual identity of the global fashion brands, encompassing layout design, fonts, colors, and general aesthetics. Any alterations needed to preserve brand consistency while modifying the design for the Shopify platform.
Cross-Device Experience
Guaranteeing a seamless and uniform user experience across all devices. The site needed to function flawlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile platforms. This encompassed uniform layouts, straightforward navigation, quick interactions, and a seamless shopping experience irrespective of screen size or device kind.
Regional Adaptation
Overseeing regional adaptation without affecting performance. The storefront needed regional adaptability, including localization and market-specific settings, while ensuring quick loading speeds and reliable performance. Modifications for the Indonesian market had to be made without compromising site speed or user experience.


Frequent updates to global sites made it challenging to keep developing the Indonesian storefront consistently aligned and up to date.


Solution Provided
Our group collaborated closely with the client to provide the subsequent solutions: Created and developed a completely customized Shopify storefront that adhered closely to international brand standards, maintaining uniformity in visuals, typography, and overall aesthetic.

Enhanced page layouts and navigation systems to facilitate user access to collections, product discovery, and purchase completion, leading to a more seamless and instinctive shopping experience.

Established region-specific settings to address localization needs, including language, currency, and market-specific configurations, allowing the storefront to expand effortlessly across various regions while maintaining performance.

To tackle this, the team synchronized the Indonesian storefront revisions according to a set scope and timeline, and collectively agreed to halt additional modifications until the global site updates were completed, ensuring stability and uniformity.
